\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Capability welfare economics has been a qualitatively rich
tradition whose measure-theoretic apparatus has remained
largely informal, even where its quantitative work is mature.
Sen's \emph{Commodities and Capabilities}~\citep{sen1985commodities}
introduces the capability set as the appropriate object for
welfare analysis but does not endow it with a measure; the modern
state of the qualitative tradition is surveyed
in~\citep{robeyns2017wellbeing}
and~\citep{chiappero2020cambridge}.  Nussbaum's
\emph{Women and Human Development}~\citep{nussbaum2000women} adds
substantive normative content but no structural axiomatization.
Becker's household-production
model~\citep{becker1965theory,becker1981treatise} specifies
utility-yielding production functions that are descriptively
flexible but predictively soft because the production functions
are essentially unconstrained.  The Stiglitz--Sen--Fitoussi
commission~\citep{stiglitz2009report} catalogues at length what
GDP-style measurement misses (non-market production, household
labour, quality-of-life capabilities) but proposes only
qualitative remedies (better surveys, satellite accounts,
multi-dimensional indices) without a unified measure-theoretic
framework on which the remedies compose.  The Alkire--Foster
multidimensional-poverty
methodology~\citep{alkire2011counting} supplies a quantitative
apparatus on capability dimensions with deprivation thresholds
and counting-based aggregation, but does not formalize
cooperative excess across capability components or the polarity
discipline that distinguishes cost-of-access from preference
revelation.

The pattern is recognizable.  Bentham's felicific
calculus, with its qualitative criteria of \emph{intensity,
duration, certainty, propinquity, fecundity, purity, extent},
was a qualitatively rich descriptive apparatus, and the expected-utility tradition (Bernoulli,
Marshall, Edgeworth, von Neumann--Morgenstern) formalized the
qualitative claims into a measure-theoretic apparatus admitting
axiomatization, falsification, and quantitative comparison.  We
position the Goal-Frontier Maximization (GFM)
sequence~\citep{lasser2026gfm,lasser2026poset,lasser2026horizon,lasser2026scm,lasser2026exo,lasser2026phase,lasser2026wamura,lasser2026paper8a,lasser2026paper8b}
as the analogous formalization for capability welfare economics.
The poset structure formalizes Sen's capability set; the volume
measures $\volL$ and $\volRwin$ formalize the capability/functioning
distinction; the residual class $\ResS$ formalizes the
shadow-economy / non-market-activity gap; the five-cell
$\delta$-decomposition formalizes the SSF beyond-GDP catalogue.
The sequence has proved its central alignment results (the
anti-monopolar property~\citep[Horizon~Aware]{lasser2026horizon},
the phase boundary~\citep[Phase~Redundancy]{lasser2026phase}, and
controlled-relaxation
convergence~\citep[Controlled~Relaxation]{lasser2026wamura}) under
the standing assumption that $\volL$ is the operational target.
The B-to-C gap, named in~\citep[Section~7]{lasser2026gfm} and
addressed at length in~\citep[Revealed~Sacrifice]{lasser2026paper8a}
and~\citep[Need~Sufficiency]{lasser2026paper8b}, is the divergence
between $\volL$ (capability \emph{possession}) and $\volR$
(capability \emph{exercise}).  Every result in the sequence
inherits this gap as a structural dependency: if the gap is large,
the guarantees the framework establishes on $\volL$ may fail to
translate to guarantees on what the collective actually does.

This paper makes the dependency formal, reading the B-to-C gap
as the central object that the formalization makes computable
and bounding the cost of the gap to alignment guarantees in the
sequence.  The formalization yields two formal contributions of
structurally distinct kinds: a \emph{containment proof}
establishing GFM as a strict superset of standard utility theory
at the linear / risk-neutral case, and an \emph{alignment bound}
formalizing the cost of proxy-instrumental optimization on GFM's
volume measure.

\begin{enumerate}
\item A \emph{fungibility-collapse correspondence theorem}
  (Section~\ref{sec:fungibility_collapse}) showing that GFM
  strictly contains standard microeconomic utility theory in its
  linear-utility / risk-neutral form: utility theory emerges as
  the special case of GFM where the capability poset reduces to a
  single purchasing-power dimension, cooperative capabilities
  vanish, and individuation becomes irrelevant.  The result is
  positioning, not an alignment guarantee: it establishes
  containment (utility theory $\subset$ GFM at $\Pstd$) plus
  extension (GFM has five structural features beyond $\Pstd$
  that utility theory lacks).  Concavity, risk-aversion, and
  heterogeneous utility require additional calibrations beyond
  the bare collapse, each a small step beyond the correspondence
  rather than a new theorem.

\item A \emph{Goodhart theorem for proxy-instrumental alignment}
  (Section~\ref{sec:goodhart}).  When the framework optimizes a
  proxy $P = \volL$ for the operational truth $T = \volRwin$, and
  $P$ is related to $T$ only instrumentally, each alignment
  property $g(\cdot)$ in the sequence, read as a Lipschitz
  function of $P$, deviates from its value under $T$ by at most
  $\mathrm{Lip}(g) \cdot \epsnonres$, where $\epsnonres$ is the
  within-channel proxy-truth disagreement gap.  This is the
  paper's load-bearing alignment result.  The property-specific
  Lipschitz analyses (anti-monopolar threshold, phase-boundary
  location, convergence rate, damage bound, aggregate-lower-bound
  slack) are perturbative roadmap items for subsequent work; this
  paper establishes the conditional transfer and identifies which
  results live in the roadmap.
\end{enumerate}

Plus a \emph{strong-form treatment} (Section~\ref{sec:lineage})
of what ``better economic models'' means structurally.  ``Better
economics'' is not one knob but four operational-intervention
channels (observation density, attestation quality, individuation
discipline, bundle decomposition), each formalizing a move the
qualitative welfare-economics tradition has been negotiating
informally, and each composing through the Goodhart machinery into
a specific alignment-tightening consequence.  The
capability-economics lineage of Sen, Nussbaum, Becker, and SSF
maps onto these channels as historical confirmation: each
qualitative tradition is a precursor whose negotiation GFM
formalizes.  Section~\ref{sec:lineage_historical} also situates
GFM relative to the broader formal-welfare-economics landscape,
including Alkire--Foster's quantitative comparator,
Bernheim--Rangel's behavioral welfare economics,
Basu--L\'opez-Calva's prior formalization survey, and the
Pattanaik--Xu / Sugden / Adler axiomatic-aggregation neighbors.
Better economic models are tighter alignment guarantees, above
the deployment-specific residual-class floor where capabilities
exercised without material or time-displacement footprint
structurally cannot be witnessed.

\subsection{What was missing from the picture}
\label{sec:whats_missing}

\citep[Revealed~Sacrifice]{lasser2026paper8a} establishes that
voluntary trade events disclose $\volR$-content through a
privacy-respecting channel, and that the constructed lower bound
$\volRlower$ on the realized exercise volume is non-decreasing in
admissibly accumulated observations.
\citep[Need~Sufficiency]{lasser2026paper8b} adds the diagnostic
architecture: the five-cell decomposition of the gap, the
wireheading-consistent concentration signal, the residual-class
characterization, and the polarity discipline that distinguishes
need-side from want-side trades.  What was missing was the formal
link between \emph{quality of the economic model} and
\emph{tightness of the alignment guarantees}.  If the framework
optimizes $\volL$ but the truth is $T$, and if $\volL$ is only
instrumentally related to $T$, then any alignment property proved
on $\volL$ need not transfer to $T$.

The Goodhart theorem of Section~\ref{sec:goodhart} makes this
transfer-or-failure formal: it gives a Lipschitz-style continuity
statement showing that alignment properties under the truth
deviate from those under the proxy in proportion to the
proxy-to-truth gap.  The B-to-C gap is therefore not a binary
precondition but a continuous gradient: what looked like a single
``does the gap close?'' question is, on inspection, a
\emph{rate-of-closure-vs-residual-floor} question that the
machinery of \citep[Revealed~Sacrifice]{lasser2026paper8a} and
\citep[Need~Sufficiency]{lasser2026paper8b} is built to answer.
The four channels of Section~\ref{sec:lineage} are how the gap
closes; the floor is what closing saturates against.

\subsection{The operational truth $T$, and the residual-class floor}
\label{sec:operational_truth}

A clarification before the central machinery.  Under
\citep[Revealed~Sacrifice]{lasser2026paper8a}'s assumption~S0
(valuation bounded by exercise: an agent's valuation $\Ui(Y)$
does not exceed the window-active realized exercise contribution
$\volRwin(Y)$), trade events provide a one-sided constraint
relating proxy disagreement to realized-exercise truth.  We adopt
$T(c) = \volRwin(c)$ as the \emph{operational} truth measure
throughout this paper.  $T$ is what
\citep[Theorem~2]{lasser2026paper8a} bounds from below via
$\volRlower$, and it is what alignment properties operate on in
practice.  The metaphysical question (``what is the agent's true
inner preference?'') is unobservable in principle, and the
framework does not pretend to access it.  S0 is the operational
bridge.

The proxy-to-truth gap has two structurally distinct components.
The \emph{within-channel disagreement}~$\epsnonres$ is the
worst-case relative deviation of the proxy from the truth on
capabilities the revealed-sacrifice channel can in principle
reach; this component is drivable downward by observation
infrastructure.  The \emph{residual-class floor}~$\epsfloor$ is
the share of operational truth that lives in capabilities
structurally outside the channel's reach (purely-private exercise
with no material \emph{and} no time-displacement footprint); this
component cannot be reduced by within-channel observation alone.
The total alignment slack is
\[
  \epsgap \;=\; \epsnonres + \lambda \cdot \epsfloor,
\]
with $\lambda \in [0,1]$ weighting the floor's contribution.

The split is structurally important and ties the alignment
programme directly to a long-standing welfare-economics problem.
Standard economics has the same residual-class
limit~\citep{stiglitz2009report}: GDP misses non-market production,
household labour, volunteer activity, gift-economy capabilities.
Imputation methods (imputed rent, time-use surveys, satellite
accounts) are the existing partial answer.  GFM does not remove
the limit; it makes the limit formally identifiable as the
structural residual class~$\ResS$
of~\citep[Definition~12]{lasser2026paper8b} with a reserved cell
($\delta_{\mathrm{residual}}$) in the gap decomposition.  The
fungibility-collapse correspondence of
Section~\ref{sec:fungibility_collapse} simplifies the poset
structure but does \emph{not} extend the channel's reach.  The
shadow-economy gap and GFM's residual class are the same
structural object viewed from different sides.

\subsection{Scope: what this paper does not do}
\label{sec:not_do}

\begin{itemize}
\item
\textbf{It does not prove the full perturbation analysis.}
This paper establishes the \emph{conditional} Lipschitz-transfer
result (Theorem~\ref{thm:goodhart}) and identifies the
alignment properties (anti-monopolar threshold, phase-boundary
location, convergence rate, damage bound, aggregate-lower-bound
slack) as the \emph{perturbative roadmap}: candidates for a
subsequent perturbation analysis.  Each property's Lipschitz
constant requires its own analysis, which is outside this
paper's scope.  We give the qualitative direction, indicate
where each constant lands, and provide concrete worked examples
of the tightening behaviour.

\item
\textbf{It does not characterize the rate of $\epsnonres$
convergence.}  ~\citep[Propositions~2~and~3]{lasser2026paper8a}
prove $\betaL$ is non-decreasing under admissible accumulation;
they do not bound \emph{how fast} $\betaL$ converges.  The rate
depends on S0 attestation quality, Part-A vs Part-B aggregation
choice, observation density, and the residual-class floor itself.
Bounding the rate is~\citep[Need~Sufficiency]{lasser2026paper8b}'s
Open~Question~6 and is the territory of subsequent perturbative
analysis; Corollary~\ref{cor:direction} of the present paper is a
direction (monotonicity), not a rate.

\item
\textbf{It does not resolve the metaphysical truth question.}
The Goodhart machinery operates on the operational
$T = \volRwin$ under S0, not on a metaphysical model of inner
preference.  Whether the agent's ``true inner preference'' (in
some philosophical sense) coincides with $\volRwin$ is outside
the formal apparatus.

\item
\textbf{It does not provide a normative capability list.}
GFM provides the structural scaffold on which normative choices
can be loaded; the choice of \emph{which} capabilities count as
central is outside the formal apparatus.  This is consistent with
Sen's stance in the original capability
approach~\citep{sen1985commodities};
Nussbaum's substantive list~\citep{nussbaum2000women,nussbaum2011creating}
is one normative loading, not the only one.

\item
\textbf{It does not unify the alignment and welfare-economics
programmes.}  The two programmes share structural content
(poset, capability measure, residual-class limit), but their
goals differ (alignment is about constraining optimization;
welfare economics is about evaluating distributions).  The
formalization claim is that both programmes have the same
structural substrate, and progress on either drives down the
same $\epsnonres$ that the other depends on; this is structural
co-progress, not unified goals.

\item
\textbf{It does not directly address Arrow's
impossibility~\citep{arrow1951social}.}
GFM measures capability-volume rather than ranking outcomes, so
the independence-of-irrelevant-alternatives axiom that drives
Arrow's impossibility does not apply to a volume measure on a
poset.  A careful argument identifying which Arrovian axioms GFM
satisfies, which it appears to sidestep at the level of Arrow's
original formulation, and where this places the framework
relative to the social-choice
literature~\citep{sen1970collective} is in
Section~\ref{sec:lineage_historical}, with the question of
whether an analogous impossibility applies to the relaxed
setting deferred to Open~Question~\ref{oq:arrow}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Summary of results}
\label{sec:results_summary}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Fungibility-collapse correspondence (containment result)}
  (Section~\ref{sec:fungibility_collapse}).
  Theorem~\ref{thm:fungibility_collapse}: an actor maximizing
  $\volL$ on the standard-economics poset $\Pstd$ is
  observationally equivalent to a linear-utility / risk-neutral
  expected-utility maximizer with the same discount factor.
  Together with the five-features catalog (non-fungibility,
  cooperative capabilities, anti-monopolar reasoning,
  individuation without interpersonal comparison, and formal
  recognition of the residual class), this establishes that GFM
  strictly contains standard utility theory at the linear / risk-
  neutral case.  Concavity, risk-aversion, and heterogeneous
  utility recover through additional calibrations.

\item \textbf{Structural-prediction class}
  (Section~\ref{sec:structural_predictions}).  Five predictions
  GFM makes that utility theory either does not, or can match
  only by adding ad hoc unfalsifiable structure: bundle-completion
  magnitude, cooperative premium, saturation transition,
  population-scale anti-monopolar (via the
  wireheading-consistent
  HHI~\citep[Proposition~6]{lasser2026paper8b}), and
  mixed-polarity bundle-for-bundle traces (via the
  bundle-decomposition extension flagged
  in~\citep[Open~Question~2]{lasser2026paper8a}).  These are what
  becomes falsifiable when capability welfare economics is
  quantified into a measure-theoretic apparatus.

\item \textbf{Goodhart theorem for proxy-instrumental alignment}
  (Section~\ref{sec:goodhart}).
  Theorem~\ref{thm:goodhart} establishes the conditional
  Lipschitz-transfer
  $|g(T) - g(P)| \leq \mathrm{Lip}(g) \cdot \epsnonres$.  This
  is the paper's novel formal contribution.  A separate
  qualitative claim (Conjecture~\ref{conj:optimization_pressure})
  states that more capable optimizers exploit larger sets of
  proxy-truth divergence regions, widening the observed
  $\epsnonres$ at equilibrium; we do not prove this conjecture,
  but the wireheading-consistent HHI is a deployment-readable
  ``concentration consistent with'' empirical witness for the
  conjectured mechanism.

\item \textbf{Strong-form direction: better economics tightens
  alignment} (Section~\ref{sec:lineage}).
  Corollary~\ref{cor:direction} states the direction
  (monotonicity); Proposition~\ref{prop:channel_composition},
  conditional on per-capability admissibility
  (Assumption~\ref{ass:per_cap_admissibility}), composes four
  operational-intervention channels into an aggregate
  alignment-slack reduction.  Channel~3 contributes through the
  $\lambda$-weighted net $\Delta_3^{\lambda} = \lambda \cdot
  \Delta_3^{\mathrm{floor}} - \Delta_3^{\mathrm{new}}$,
  capturing the floor reduction from re-individuation against
  the new within-channel ceiling contribution from the
  newly-individuated capability.  The lineage subsection
  (Section~\ref{sec:lineage_historical}) maps each precursor onto
  the channels: the qualitative-tradition figures (Sen, Nussbaum,
  Becker, SSF, Arrow) supply the moves the channels formalize,
  and the already-quantitative comparator (Alkire--Foster
  multidimensional poverty measurement,
  \citep{alkire2011counting}) supplies the closest existing
  apparatus that GFM extends with cooperative excess and polarity
  discipline.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Design principle}
\label{sec:design_principle}

This paper inherits the structural discipline of the preceding
sequence papers and applies it one level up.
\citep[Phase~Redundancy]{lasser2026phase}
and~\citep[Controlled~Relaxation]{lasser2026wamura} gain
precision through frequency rather than depth (more tests, not
more-intrusive tests).
\citep[Revealed~Sacrifice]{lasser2026paper8a} gains
exercise-volume precision through frequency rather than depth
(more trade observations, not more-intrusive trader surveillance).
The present paper inherits a meta-version of the same discipline:
\emph{the quality of the economic model controls the tightness of
the alignment guarantees, and the quality of the economic model
is itself a question of channel reach and observation density},
not of more-intrusive measurement.  The proxy-to-truth gap is
closed by observation infrastructure operating within the
framework's existing privacy commitments, not by relaxing those
commitments.
