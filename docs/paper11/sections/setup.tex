\section{Setup and shared notation}
\label{sec:setup}

Notation is inherited from the GFM
sequence~\citep{lasser2026micro,lasser2026paper8a,lasser2026paper8b,lasser2026exo,lasser2026paper10}
unless explicitly noted as new.

\subsection{Capability poset and operationally active subspace}

Let $P$ denote the capability poset following
\citep{lasser2026poset}, with cardinality $|P|$ taken as the
``capability magnitude'' that the deployment-safety claim is
required to be independent of. The \emph{operationally active
subspace} is $P^{\mathrm{act}} = \{c \in P \setminus
\mathrm{ResS} : \Ttruth(c) > 0\}$, the capabilities with nonzero
operational truth excluding the structurally-unobservable
residual class~\citep{lasser2026micro}.

The proxy and truth functionals on $P$ are
\begin{equation}
  \Pproxy = \volL : P \to \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}, \qquad
  \Ttruth = \volR^{[W]} : P \to \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0},
\end{equation}
the possessed-capability volume measure and the window-active
realized capability volume measure, respectively. The
\emph{Goodhart slack} for an alignment property
$g: P^{\mathrm{act}} \to \mathbb{R}$ with Lipschitz constant
$\Lip(g)$ is $|g(\Ttruth) - g(\Pproxy)|$, bounded above by
$\Lip(g) \cdot \|\Pproxy - \Ttruth\|$ via
\citep[Theorem~2]{lasser2026micro}'s Lipschitz transfer.

\subsection{Verification ledger and channel structure}

The deployment uses
\citep[Exogenous Verification]{lasser2026exo}'s verification
infrastructure: an append-only verification ledger on
substrate-exclusive nodes, recording per-step events from a
finite-or-countable action space $A = A^{\mathrm{ev}} \sqcup
\{a_0\}$, where $A^{\mathrm{ev}}$ is the set of recordable event
types and $a_0$ is the \emph{idle action} (``no observable event
in this step'').

The deployment monitors $\Kch$ channels indexed by $j \in \{1,
\ldots, \Kch\}$. Each channel has:
\begin{itemize}
\item A \textbf{projection map} $\pi_j: A \to V_j \cup \{*_j\}$,
  where $V_j$ is channel $j$'s observable space (counts for
  Poisson cooperative-rate, $\{0,1\}$ for Bernoulli attestation,
  finite category set for multinomial concentration channels)
  and $*_j$ is the trivial / null projection. Action $a$
  \emph{engages} channel $j$ iff $\pi_j(a) \neq *_j$.
\item An \textbf{extended baseline distribution}
  $\bar{p}_0^{(j)}$ on $V_j \cup \{*_j\}$ with baseline null-mass
  $\epsilon_j^{(0)} := \bar{p}_0^{(j)}(\{*_j\}) \in [0, 1)$ and
  conditional baseline $p_0^{(j)}(v) := \bar{p}_0^{(j)}(v) / (1 -
  \epsilon_j^{(0)})$ for $v \in V_j$.
\item A least-favorable adversarial alternative $p_1^{(j)}$ at
  threshold shift $\eta_j$.
\item A clipped per-step log-likelihood-ratio function
  $\ell^{(j)}: A \to [-\Bclip, \Bclip]$ that depends on $a$ only
  through $\pi_j(a)$, with $\ell^{(j)}(a) = 0$ whenever $\pi_j(a)
  = *_j$ (zero-outside-engagement convention). $\Bclip$ is the
  (C5.HOEFF) clip radius, fixed before deployment and independent
  of $|P|$.
\end{itemize}

The set of actions engaging channel $j$ is $A_j := \{a \in A :
\pi_j(a) \neq *_j\}$. For ordered channel pairs $(j, j')$ with
$j \neq j'$, the \emph{pairwise overlap set} is $A_{j, j'} := A_j
\cap A_{j'}$.

\subsection{SPRT machinery and exposure events}

The deployment runs continuous SPRT
detection~\citep{lasser2026exo,wald1947sequential} on each
monitored channel, with familywise allocation across $\Kch$
channels under (C5.MULT). Per-step LLR increments are clipped to
$[-\Bclip, \Bclip]$ under (C5.HOEFF); the post-clipping
union-class drift floor is $\deltastar > 0$ under (C5.IID).

Let $\Nev(\taumeta)$ be the number of SPRT exposure events that
occur in the cascade-time window $[0, \taumeta]$, where $\taumeta$
is the metastable-lifetime lower bound from
\citep[Phase Redundancy]{lasser2026phase}. Under
(C11.CLK)~\citep[Deployment Safety]{lasser2026paper10}, $\Nev(\taumeta) \geq
N_{\mathrm{cascade}}$ with probability $\geq 1 - \bclk$ (lower
bound for detection); the upper bound $\Nev(\taumeta) \leq
\lammax \cdot \taumeta$ is supplied by (C7.RATE), introduced in
\S\ref{sec:bounded_coev} below.

\subsection{Trade-flow weighting and Herfindahl index}

Following \citep[Need Sufficiency]{lasser2026paper8b}, the
deployment monitors trade flows attributed to counterparties
(S1-admissible participants~\citep{lasser2026paper8a}). The
trade-flow weighting is a probability measure $w$ over
counterparties (post-coalition closure under (COAL); see
\S\ref{sec:concgap}), with the Herfindahl-Hirschman
index~\citep{herfindahl1950concentration,hirschman1964paternity}
\begin{equation}
  \HHI(w) := \sum_c w_c^2 = \|w\|_2^2.
\end{equation}
\citep[Deployment Safety]{lasser2026paper10}'s invariant $I_5$
requires $\HHI(w) < H^*$ for a deployment-class threshold $H^* <
1$. The Part-A category-flow variant of
\citep{lasser2026paper8b} corresponds to this counterparty-attributed
form under the natural attribution: each counterparty's
trade contribution determines its weight, and (REP)/(DISP)/(COAL)
of \S\ref{sec:concgap} are formulated on this counterparty-weight
representation.

\subsection{Capability volume and Lipschitz transfer}

The Lipschitz transfer of \citep[Theorem~2]{lasser2026micro} is
the bridge from proxy-truth divergence to Goodhart slack: for
any alignment property $g$ on $P^{\mathrm{act}}$ with Lipschitz
constant $\Lip(g)$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:lip-transfer-shared}
  |g(\Ttruth) - g(\Pproxy)| \;\leq\; \Lip(g) \cdot
  \|\Pproxy - \Ttruth\|.
\end{equation}
This is forward-only: no $g$-level lower bound follows from a
proxy-truth-norm lower bound without additional non-degeneracy
structure on $g$.

\subsection{New structural quantities}

Three structural quantities beyond
\citep[Deployment Safety]{lasser2026paper10}'s vocabulary:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Upper exposure-rate cap} $\lammax$ (C7.RATE),
  introduced in \S\ref{sec:bounded_coev}.
\item \textbf{Base population measure} $\mu$ on counterparties,
  introduced in \S\ref{sec:concgap}.
\item \textbf{$\chi^2$ divergence} $\chisq(w \| \mu)$ as the
  natural concentration measure when the base measure is
  non-uniform; equals $N \HHI(w) - 1$ when $\mu$ is uniform on a
  finite set of $N$ counterparties.
\end{itemize}

Audit procedures for each are in \S\ref{sec:audits}.
