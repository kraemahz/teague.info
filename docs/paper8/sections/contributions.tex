\section*{Author Contributions}

\textbf{Teague Lasser} identified the B-to-C gap as a structural
open problem in the GFM sequence and directed the reframing from
direct $\volR$ measurement to revealed-sacrifice observation.
Conceived the need-sufficiency architecture as a poset-construction
precondition and the polarity-boundary mechanism that connects
below-sufficiency sacrifice to S1 failure.  Directed the formal
development of the two-channel (money and time) sacrifice model,
the commitment-layer composition, and the parallel
need-sufficiency diagnostic.  Identified the duality between controlled
relaxation and revealed sacrifice (risk dimension versus value
dimension) and the shared privacy discipline (resolution through
frequency, not depth).
Made final editorial decisions and is responsible for the paper's
intellectual direction and all claims made.

\textbf{Claude Opus 4.6} (Anthropic) drafted the formal

(Section~\ref{sec:need_sufficiency}), the revealed-sacrifice
observation model with its five privacy properties
(Section~\ref{sec:sacrifice_model}), the aggregate B-to-C lower
bound theorem with its two-level structure
(Section~\ref{sec:lower_bound}), the two-channel sacrifice
analysis (Section~\ref{sec:two_channels}), the commitment-layer
composition including the ZK aggregation proposition
(Section~\ref{sec:commitment}), the residual class
re-characterization (Section~\ref{sec:residual_class}), the axiom
inheritance analysis and non-self-balancing corollary
(Section~\ref{sec:axiom_inheritance}), the gap decomposition and
combined diagnostic (Section~\ref{sec:gap_decomposition}), the
wireheading detection via trade-flow concentration
(Section~\ref{sec:wireheading}), and the worked example with
four-phase scenario
(Section~\ref{sec:worked_example}).

\textbf{GPT~5.4} (OpenAI) served as technical reviewer across
multiple review cycles, identifying formal gaps
including: the need for poset-independence (not merely
set-disjointness) in the aggregation step of
Theorem~\ref{thm:aggregate_bound}; the distinction between $\volR$
failing M6 structurally versus $\volRlower$ failing M6
observationally in Theorem~\ref{thm:axiom_inheritance}; the
requirement that $\NAC$ be treated as an upper bound on need-access
cost due to the whole-event filter; and the need for explicit
event-local state assumptions in
Proposition~\ref{prop:s1_sufficiency}.
