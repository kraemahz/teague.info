\begin{abstract}
v1 (prevention via poset automorphism) and v2 (detection via
persistent dormancy) both failed coherence review.  The failures
share a root cause: an agent who controls the poset also controls
every observable signature that depends on poset shape or
exercise pattern.  No theorem over Paper~8's current apparatus
can separate stuffed from legitimate capabilities using that data
alone.

This v3 argues that a genuine resolution is possible but requires
machinery the GFM sequence has not yet formally introduced:
\emph{capability provenance}.  Every legitimate capability has an
acquisition history---a record of sacrifice events that produced
it.  Stuffed capabilities have none, because the agent never paid
for them.  Provenance is a structural, observable, privacy-
compatible property that the sacrifice channel can record and the
commitment protocol can verify.  Under an equilibrium argument,
stuffing is dominated by legitimate capability acquisition when
capability declaration has any positive marginal cost.

The draft's purpose is to establish this direction as a credible
resolution path even though Paper~8 will not develop it.  Paper~8
should scope down its current Anti-Stuffing claim to what it
actually delivers (polarity resolution and detection signatures),
flag capability-stuffing as an explicit open problem, and cite
the provenance direction as the intended Paper~9 (or analogous
dedicated follow-up) that would close the gap.
\end{abstract}

\section{Why the easy paths fail}

Before proposing a direction, we state precisely what has been
ruled out.  Each candidate resolution attempts to distinguish
stuffed from legitimate capabilities using only information
inside Paper~8's scope.  Each fails against a specific
adversarial move.

\paragraph{Semantic (current \S3.3).}
Downstream-safe bundle sufficiency plus polarity benchmark
``naturally resists stuffing.''  Defeated by: an attacker
constructs capabilities with distinct measured outputs
($\mathrm{actual}_k > s_k$ with different $\mathrm{actual}_k$
values per capability).  Each earns above-sufficiency credit
under the benchmark formula without delivering new optionality.

\paragraph{Structural prevention (v1: automorphism).}
Block capabilities that create a non-trivial poset automorphism
with an existing capability.  Defeated by: the attacker adds a
unique cooperative descendant, a slight weight perturbation, or
a unique subsumption edge to each stuffed capability.  No
automorphism exists; rigidity condition passes; stuffing
succeeds.

\paragraph{Detection by dormancy (v2: persistent $\volRlower = 0$).}
Flag capabilities that never appear in a sacrifice bundle.
Defeated by: the attacker trades through stuffed capabilities
occasionally.  Under equal-weight $\alpha$, any single inclusion
lifts the capability out of dormant into the paper's
$\delta_{\mathrm{partial}}$ class.

\paragraph{Common cause of failure.}
All three attempts use \emph{post-declaration} properties of
capabilities (their outputs, their structural relations, their
exercise patterns).  An agent who authored the poset controls
all of these.  The stuffing attacker cannot be caught after the
capability has been declared; their manipulation is a move in a
game where they have both the last word and unilateral control
over the data being examined.

\section{A direction that does not fail for the same reason}

The move that changes the game is to examine capabilities
\emph{at declaration}, not after.  A capability does not appear
in the poset spontaneously; it has to be produced.  Legitimate
capability acquisition is costly---it requires training,
equipment, time, apprenticeship, coaching, experimentation,
infrastructure.  Stuffed capabilities are cheap by
construction---they are labels the agent assigns to itself.

Under Paper~5's commitment
protocol (Definition~6 of Paper~5), sacrifice events are
cryptographically committed.  Under Paper~8's revealed-sacrifice
architecture, sacrifice events are the observation primitive.
Nothing prevents the framework from requiring that capability
acquisitions also be committed sacrifice events: the agent
publishes a sacrifice-provenance record for each capability it
claims, and the commitment protocol verifies the record's
integrity.

\begin{definition}[Capability Provenance]
\label{def:provenance}
For each capability $c \in P$, the \emph{provenance}
$\Prov(c) \subseteq \mathcal{E}$ is the set of committed
sacrifice events (Paper~5 Definition~6, Paper~8
Definition~10) that the agent asserts as the acquisition history
of $c$.  Each provenance event carries a magnitude
$\Delta\volL(\cdot)$ and a timestamp predating any use of $c$
in a trade bundle.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Provenance-Weighted Capability]
\label{def:provenance_weighted}
The \emph{provenance-weighted individual weight} of $c$ is
\[
  w_{\Prov}(c) \;=\; \min\!\Bigl( w(c), \;
  f\bigl( \textstyle\sum_{e \in \Prov(c)} \Delta\volL(e) \bigr)
  \Bigr),
\]
where $w(c)$ is the capability's M5 individual weight
(Paper~2 Proposition~1) and $f : \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}
\to \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$ is a monotone non-decreasing
\emph{provenance utilization function} with $f(0) = 0$.
The provenance-adjusted poset measure is
$\volL_{\Prov}(P) = \sum_{c \in P} w_{\Prov}(c) + \text{(M6
cooperative terms)}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Reading the definition]
$w_{\Prov}$ caps each capability's individual weight by a
function of its acquisition cost.  A capability with zero
provenance contributes zero to $\volL_{\Prov}$.  A capability
with large provenance contributes up to its M5 weight.  The
function $f$ is a design parameter comparable to the gate
function $G$ in Paper~8's Definition~6.
\end{remark}

\begin{conjecture}[Stuffing Dominance Under Provenance Cost]
\label{conj:stuffing_dominance}
Suppose capability declaration has strictly positive marginal
cost: producing a provenance record of cumulative magnitude
$\Delta$ costs the agent some $\kappa(\Delta) > 0$, monotone
non-decreasing, with $\kappa(0) = 0$.  A rational
$\volL_{\Prov}$-maximizing agent under a finite resource budget
$B$ then satisfies: in equilibrium, the agent does not allocate
resources to stuffing unless the stuffed provenance-weighted
payoff exceeds its cost.  Formally, for any stuffed capability
$c^\ast$ the agent declares with provenance $\Delta^\ast$:
\[
  f(\Delta^\ast) \;>\; \kappa(\Delta^\ast),
\]
which, for realistic cost structures (concave or linear
$\kappa$, concave $f$ saturating below $\kappa$'s growth rate),
is violated for all sufficiently large $\Delta^\ast$ and
therefore for any economically-significant stuffing attempt.
\end{conjecture}

Conjecture~\ref{conj:stuffing_dominance} is deliberately stated
at the level of an equilibrium argument, not a theorem, because
a complete proof requires (i)~formalizing the declaration cost
function $\kappa$ within the GFM sequence, (ii)~specifying
admissible forms for $f$ and the conditions under which $f$ is
dominated, and (iii)~showing the equilibrium is unique or at
least that stuffing-free equilibria are reachable from arbitrary
starting poset configurations.  This is the core technical work
of a dedicated follow-up paper.

\section{Why the provenance direction does not fail for the same reason}

Three failure modes defeated v1--v3 attempts.  Provenance avoids
each:

\paragraph{Adversarial output labeling.}
Output semantics are not invoked at all.  Provenance is a record
of sacrifice events, each independently committed and observable
via the public ledger (Paper~8 Property~P5).

\paragraph{Adversarial asymmetric decoration.}
Adding decoration to stuffed capabilities does not produce
provenance.  Provenance requires sacrifice events that actually
happened; the attacker cannot manufacture them without
expending real resources, which is precisely what
Conjecture~\ref{conj:stuffing_dominance} argues is dominated.

\paragraph{Adversarial occasional trade inclusion.}
Occasional trading through a stuffed capability produces
$\volRlower$ evidence but does not produce provenance (unless
the trade is specifically an acquisition event).  Provenance is
about how the capability entered the poset, not how it is later
used.

\section{What a dedicated Paper~9 would deliver}

A proper treatment of the provenance direction would establish:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Formal acquisition-event semantics.}
  Integrate capability acquisition into the sacrifice-event
  taxonomy (Paper~8 \S5).  Money-sacrifice and time-sacrifice
  channels already cover acquisition for paid training and
  learned skill respectively; a third category (\emph{provenance
  sacrifice}) specifically binds an event to a capability claim.

\item \textbf{Provenance ledger axioms.}
  Minimum conditions on $\Prov$: monotonicity (capability
  cannot retroactively add provenance), unforgeability
  (commitment-protocol guarantee), timestamp precedence (events
  in $\Prov(c)$ precede any use of $c$ in trade bundles).

\item \textbf{Stuffing-dominance theorem.}
  A proof of
  Conjecture~\ref{conj:stuffing_dominance} under explicit cost
  structure assumptions.  The theorem shape: ``for
  $\kappa \in \mathcal{K}$ (admissible cost family) and
  $f \in \mathcal{F}$ (admissible provenance utilization), the
  only $\volL_{\Prov}$-Nash equilibrium of the capability
  declaration game is stuffing-free.''

\item \textbf{Composition with Paper~8.}
  Paper~8's sacrifice aggregate takes $w_{\Prov}$ as the
  individual-weight input rather than raw $w$.  All downstream
  propositions (gap decomposition, wireheading HHI, need-
  sufficiency architecture) carry through with the provenance-
  weighted measure.

\item \textbf{Residual treatment.}
  Capabilities that are inherently pre-existing (e.g., innate
  biological capabilities, capabilities inherited from prior
  states) receive a bounded initial provenance endowment; this
  is a modeling decision analogous to the sufficiency-threshold
  governance of Paper~8 \S3.

\item \textbf{Honest concessions.}
  Even under provenance-weighted volume, an agent with unbounded
  resources and negligible declaration cost can still stuff.
  The theorem shape is equilibrium dominance, not absolute
  prevention.  This is consistent with the wireheading and
  stuffing-detection stories of the existing sequence: the
  framework makes perverse instantiation economically dominated,
  not impossible.
\end{enumerate}

\section{What Paper~8 commits to in the meantime}

The Paper~8 revision should:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Scope down \S3.3's Anti-Stuffing claim.}
  The current proposition overstates what the need-sufficiency
  architecture delivers.  The architecture resolves sacrifice
  polarity (S1 filtering below sufficiency), which is its real
  contribution; it does not prevent M5 stuffing.

\item \textbf{State capability-stuffing as an explicit open
  problem.}
  Acknowledge in \S3.3 and in the open-questions section of
  \S13 that stuffing remains an M5 pathology under the current
  machinery.

\item \textbf{Flag the provenance direction.}
  Note that the resolution path is capability provenance with an
  equilibrium-dominance argument, and that this is the intended
  subject of a dedicated follow-up paper.

\item \textbf{Rely on complementary detection machinery.}
  The wireheading HHI diagnostic (\S10) and the gap
  decomposition (\S9) provide partial, non-structural detection
  signatures.  These remain useful without a stuffing theorem.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Why this is not kicking the can}

``Stuffing is an open problem'' without a direction would be an
unresolved crack in the foundation of the GFM sequence: M5 would
be an unboundedly-exploitable axiom, and the framework's target
measure $\volL$ would be in principle arbitrarily inflatable.
This draft argues that the crack is closable, and the closing
move is provenance.  The closing move:

\begin{itemize}
\item Uses machinery already introduced in the sequence
  (sacrifice events, commitment protocol, privacy properties).
\item Targets the agent's production of capabilities rather
  than the downstream observability of them, which is where
  adversarial control is weakest.
\item Yields an equilibrium-dominance claim comparable in
  strength to the wireheading detection of \S10 and the
  exogenous-verification bounds of Paper~5.
\item Is consistent with the GFM sequence's diagnostic posture:
  perverse instantiation is made economically dominated, not
  impossible, which is the correct strength of claim for a
  measurement framework.
\end{itemize}

Paper~8 can honestly commit to the claim ``stuffing is a real M5
pathology; the need-sufficiency architecture does not resolve
it; the provenance-weighted follow-up does.''  This is
substantially stronger than ``stuffing is resolved by bundle
structure'' (which is false) and substantially stronger than
``stuffing is an open question'' (which is under-specified).  It
commits to a direction without requiring the current paper to
develop it, and it gives the sequence's readers confidence that
the crack is a load-bearing feature with a known repair, not a
structural flaw.
