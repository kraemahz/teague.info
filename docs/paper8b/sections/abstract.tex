\begin{abstract}
The companion paper~\citep{lasser2026paper8a} proves the
\emph{Aggregate B-to-C Lower Bound} theorem for a
privacy-respecting observation channel on realized capability
volume $\volR$, under admissibility conditions that include S1
(free choice with non-degrading alternatives).  This paper
supplies the structural architecture that gives a
sufficient filter condition (subject to externally attested
hypotheses and governance-supplied thresholds) for the
below-sufficiency failure mode of S1, leaving other S1 failure
modes (coercion, duress) as residual filtering
responsibilities of the institutional attestation layer.  It
also develops the diagnostic layers that interpret the bound's
output.

The \emph{need-sufficiency architecture} is a poset-construction
precondition distinguishing involuntary need-satisfaction from
voluntary want-pursuit via multi-dimensional sufficiency
thresholds and downstream-safe gates.  Below-sufficiency
sacrifices in which retention is degrading and no third
non-degrading alternative exists are structurally involuntary;
under that narrow condition S1 fails
(Proposition~\ref{prop:s1_sufficiency}) and the events are
filtered before reaching the observation channel.  A parallel
\emph{need-access cost} diagnostic tracks below-sufficiency
expenditure as a companion metric.

The \emph{gap decomposition} partitions the B-to-C gap into
four disjoint cells (restricted, covered, dormant, residual)
under poset-boundary closure---with an optional fifth bucket
($\delta_{\mathrm{boundary}}$) when the boundary-residual
closure variant is selected, used in the worked example to
preserve seed-cell attribution.  The cell-attribution
\emph{classification step} is computable in near-linear
$\widetilde{O}(|P|+E+M)$ time given upstream inputs
(admissibility labels, residual-class membership,
restriction set, sufficiency thresholds), which are themselves
external attestations or governance-supplied structural data
rather than computed by this step.  A
\emph{wireheading-consistent concentration signal} from the
trade-flow Herfindahl--Hirschman Index is Schur-convex in the
majorization order; the Part-A category-flow variant is
third-party observable from public committed events.  The
\emph{residual class} is re-characterized from a
measurement-apparatus question to a channel-reach question,
yielding the decomposition's structurally-distinct component
(irreducible under the residual-isolation hygiene check
described in Section~\ref{sec:gap_decomposition}).  A
four-phase worked example demonstrates the architecture
end-to-end.
\end{abstract}
