\begin{abstract}
The companion paper~\citep{lasser2026paper8a} proves the
\emph{Aggregate B-to-C Lower Bound} theorem for a
privacy-respecting observation channel on realized capability
volume $\volR$, under admissibility conditions including S1
(free choice with non-degrading alternatives).  This paper
supplies the structural condition that makes S1's
below-sufficiency failure mode diagnosable (the actual
filtering remains an institutional-attestation step; the
condition identifies which events to filter, not by itself a
filtering computation), together with the diagnostic
architecture that interprets the bound's output.

The \emph{need-sufficiency architecture} is a
poset-construction precondition distinguishing involuntary
need-satisfaction from voluntary want-pursuit via
multi-dimensional sufficiency thresholds and downstream-safe
gates.  Definition~\ref{def:s1_sufficiency} establishes a
sufficient condition under which S1 fails (retention degrades
the agent below threshold and no third non-degrading
alternative exists), filtering such events from the aggregate.
A parallel \emph{need-access cost} diagnostic tracks
below-sufficiency expenditure as a companion metric.

The \emph{gap decomposition} partitions the B-to-C gap into
four cells (restricted, covered, dormant, residual) under
poset-boundary closure (cooperative-closure semantics,
strategies (a)/(b)) or into five cells with an additional
boundary-residual bucket under the literal-partition variant
(strategy (c), used in the worked example to preserve
seed-cell attribution); the choice of closure strategy is
documented in Remark~\ref{rem:cell_cooperative_closure}.
Cell classification is computable in near-linear
$\widetilde{O}(|P|+E+M)$ time given external admissibility
labels and governance inputs.  A
\emph{wireheading-consistent concentration signal} from the
trade-flow Herfindahl--Hirschman Index is Schur-convex in the
majorization order; the Part-A category-flow variant is
third-party observable from public committed events.  The
\emph{residual class} is re-characterized from a
measurement-apparatus question to a channel-reach question.
A four-phase worked example demonstrates the architecture
end-to-end.
\end{abstract}
