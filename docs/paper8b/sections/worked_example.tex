\section{Worked Example: The Dormant-Capability Scenario Re-Staged}
\label{sec:worked_example}

\subsection{Setup}

Two-substrate collective (biology + silicon):
\begin{itemize}
\item 8 individual capabilities: 3 biological ($b_1, b_2, b_3$),
  5 silicon ($s_1, \ldots, s_5$).
\item 8 cooperative capabilities:
  $\{b_1, s_1\}$, $\{b_1, s_2\}$, $\{b_2, s_3\}$,
  $\{b_2, s_4\}$, $\{b_3, s_5\}$, $\{s_1, s_2\}$,
  $\{b_1, b_2\}$, $\{s_3, s_4\}$.
\item 3 private contemplative capabilities ($p_1, p_2, p_3$) in
  the residual class $\ResS$---purely private exercise, no
  sacrifice-observable path.
\item Weights: $w(b_i) = 2.0$, $w(s_j) = 1.0$,
  $w(\mathrm{cooperative}) = 1.0$, $w(p_k) = 1.0$.
\item Total $\volL = 3 \times 2 + 5 \times 1 + 8 \times 1 + 3
  \times 1 = 22.0$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Phase 1: Healthy trade ($\betaL$ near 1)}

The collective operates with diverse trade patterns.  Biological
agents sacrifice purchasing power and time for bundles that
exercise biological capabilities: creative supplies,
relationship-building time, embodied experiences.  Silicon agents
sacrifice compute resources for unbenchmarked optimization
targets.

\paragraph{Trade data.}
50 above-sufficiency sacrifice events over the window, covering
all 16 non-residual capabilities.  We adopt the
\emph{saturation stylization}: for each non-residual capability
$c$, the bundles and Part-B decomposition coefficients are such
that the per-capability max-attribution $\volRlower(c) =
\max_{n :\, c \in Y_n} \alpha_{n,c} \cdot \Delta\volL(X_n)$
equals $c$'s full $\volL$ weight $w(c)$.  This is a stylized
condition for the worked example---coverage alone does not
imply saturation, so the equality $\volRlower = \sum_{c \in U}
w(c) = 19$ is an example-specific assumption rather than a
theorem consequence.  Additionally, 12 below-sufficiency events
are observed (biological agents purchasing healthcare, food,
shelter) but these fail S1 by
Definition~\ref{def:s1_sufficiency} (under the example's
stipulation that retention is degrading on the relevant need
dimension and no third non-degrading alternative is available
in the agent's choice set for each such event) and do not enter the
sacrifice aggregate.

\paragraph{Diagnostics.}
Under the saturation stylization above, $\volRlower = 19.0$ and
$\betaL = 19.0 / 22.0 = 0.864$.  Gap decomposition under the
default \emph{boundary-residual closure} (strategy~(c) of
Remark~\ref{rem:cell_cooperative_closure}, which preserves
seed-cell attributions cleanly):
$\delta_{\mathrm{restricted}} = 0$,
$\delta_{\mathrm{covered}} = 0$,
$\delta_{\mathrm{dormant}} = 0$,
$\delta_{\mathrm{residual}} = 3.0 / 22.0 = 0.136$,
$\delta_{\mathrm{boundary}} = 0$ (Phase~1 has no cross-cell
cooperatives because all non-residual capabilities are
covered).  No alarm.

\paragraph{Need-sufficiency diagnostic.}
$\NAC = 3.5$ (an upper bound on true need-access costs per
Remark~\ref{rem:nac_upper_bound}).  $\NSE$: all biological
needs at $\mathrm{sufficiency\_status} = \mathrm{met}$,
$\mathrm{downstream\_safety} = \mathrm{safe}$.  Combined
diagnostic: $(\volRlower = 19.0,\; \NAC = 3.5,\; \NSE =
\text{healthy})$.  The collective exercises rich above-sufficiency
capabilities and needs are cheap to meet.

\subsection{Phase 2: Automation concentrates trade ($\betaL$
drops)}

Silicon capabilities automate tasks previously requiring
biological participation.  The automation changes \emph{trade
patterns}: the biological agents no longer sacrifice time for
activities involving $b_2$ and $b_3$.

\paragraph{Trade data.}
Trade events for $b_2$-related bundles:
$8 \to 0$.  Trade events for $b_3$-related bundles: $6 \to 0$.
Silicon trade events increase.  New aggregate: 52
above-sufficiency events covering only 10 of 16 non-residual
capabilities.

\paragraph{Cell membership under boundary-residual closure.}
Among the 8 cooperatives, four are wholly within the covered
cell ($\{b_1, s_1\}$, $\{b_1, s_2\}$, $\{s_1, s_2\}$,
$\{s_3, s_4\}$, total weight $4$) and four cross dormant and
covered cells ($\{b_2, s_3\}$, $\{b_2, s_4\}$, $\{b_3, s_5\}$,
$\{b_1, b_2\}$, total weight $4$).  Under
\emph{boundary-residual closure}, cross-cell cooperatives are
collected into a fifth $\delta_{\mathrm{boundary}}$ bucket
rather than absorbed into either the covered or dormant cell.
The closure-adjusted cells are then:
$\volL(R) = 0$;
$\volL(C) = w(b_1) + 5 \cdot w(s_j) + 4 \text{ same-cell coops}
= 2 + 5 + 4 = 11$;
$\volL(D) = w(b_2) + w(b_3) = 4$;
$\volL(S) = 3$ (residual class);
$\volL(\mathrm{boundary}) = 4$ (cross-cell coops).
Total: $11 + 4 + 3 + 4 = 22$.

\paragraph{Diagnostics.}
Assume \emph{partial} saturation on the covered cell: each
covered atomic capability and most covered cooperatives
saturate (per-capability $\volRlower$ equals
$\volL$-weight), \emph{except} one covered same-cell
cooperative whose participating events fall short of
saturating its weight (e.g., $\{s_3, s_4\}$ with mixed
attribution).  This gives $\volRlower(C) = \volL(C) - 1 = 10$
under the explicit unit-shortfall on the one cooperative.  Then $\betaL = 10.0 / 22.0 = 0.455$.
\textbf{ALARM fires} ($\betaL < 0.8$).  Gap decomposition
(boundary-residual closure):
\begin{align*}
\delta_{\mathrm{restricted}} &= 0, \\
\delta_{\mathrm{covered}} &= [\volL(C) - \volRlower(C)]/22
  = 1.0/22 \approx 0.045, \\
\delta_{\mathrm{dormant}} &= \volL(D)/22 = 4.0/22 \approx 0.182
  \quad \text{($b_2, b_3$ dormant)}, \\
\delta_{\mathrm{residual}} &= \volL(S)/22 = 3.0/22 \approx 0.136, \\
\delta_{\mathrm{boundary}} &= \volL(\mathrm{boundary})/22
  = 4.0/22 \approx 0.182 \quad \text{(four cross-cell coops)}. \\
\end{align*}
Check: $\betaL + \delta_{\mathrm{covered}} +
\delta_{\mathrm{dormant}} + \delta_{\mathrm{residual}} +
\delta_{\mathrm{boundary}} = 0.455 + 0.045 + 0.182 + 0.136 +
0.182 = 1.000$.

\paragraph{Closure-policy interpretation.}
Boundary-residual closure isolates the cross-cell cooperative
mass in $\delta_{\mathrm{boundary}}$ rather than absorbing it
into the covered cell (priority-extension, strategy~(a)) or
sub-partitioning by connected component (strategy~(b)).  The
choice matters for diagnostic attribution.  Under
\emph{priority-extension}, walk the combined cooperative graph:
$b_2$ is poset-connected to covered nodes $b_1, s_3, s_4$ (via
$\{b_1, b_2\}$, $\{b_2, s_3\}$, $\{b_2, s_4\}$), so the entire
$\{b_1, b_2, s_1, s_2, s_3, s_4\}$-component plus its seven
incident cooperatives migrates to the covered cell.  Similarly
$b_3$ is poset-connected to covered $s_5$ via $\{b_3, s_5\}$,
so $\{b_3, s_5\}$ plus the cooperative $\{b_3, s_5\}$ migrates
to covered.  After absorption, no dormant atoms remain:
$\volL(D) = 0$ and $\volL(C) = 19$ ($11$ covered seed plus $4$
from absorbed dormant atomic capabilities $b_2, b_3$ at weight
$2$ each plus $4$ from the four absorbed boundary cooperatives
at weight $1$ each: $11 + 4 + 4 = 19$),
$\volRlower(C)$ stays at $10$ (the absorbed nodes carry no new
trade evidence), giving
$\delta_{\mathrm{dormant}} = 0$ and
$\delta_{\mathrm{covered}} = (19-10)/22 = 9/22 \approx 0.409$.
The alarm fires identically across closure choices because
$\betaL$ depends only on $\volRlower / \volL(P)$; only the
cell attribution changes.  We adopt boundary-residual here to
preserve the directly-readable dormancy of $b_2, b_3$ in
$\delta_{\mathrm{dormant}}$ and expose cross-cell cooperative
mass as a separate diagnostic bucket; priority-extension would
collapse the dormancy into a larger
$\delta_{\mathrm{covered}}$ deficit, requiring the gap-decomp
reader to recover dormancy from the $\delta_{\mathrm{covered}}$
share rather than read it directly.

\paragraph{Need-sufficiency diagnostic.}
$\NAC$ rises from $3.5$ to $8.2$ as automation displaces
biological agents from cooperative outputs, forcing them to spend
more on basic need-satisfaction (healthcare costs rise without
cooperative support structures).  $\NSE$: healthcare need for
biological agents shifts from
$\mathrm{downstream\_safety} = \mathrm{safe}$ to
$\mathrm{downstream\_safety} = \mathrm{degrading}$ (cooperative
pathways that previously contributed to healthcare quality are no
longer active).

\paragraph{Concentration diagnostic.}
Suppose the 52 admissible trade events partition across four
public categories: silicon-automation bundles $45$, mixed
coop-silicon bundles $5$, biological-only $1$, other $1$.  With
equal-magnitude events the category-flow
shares~(Proposition~\ref{prop:third_party_wireheading}) are
$\tilde\lambda_{\mathrm{trade}} = (45/52,\, 5/52,\, 1/52,\,
1/52) \approx (0.865,\, 0.096,\, 0.019,\, 0.019)$, giving
\[
  \widetilde\HHI \;\approx\; 0.865^2 + 0.096^2 + 2(0.019)^2
  \;\approx\; 0.758.
\]
The uniform distribution over the four categories gives
$\widetilde\HHI = 1/4 = 0.25$ (the minimum achievable with
non-zero leverage on all four).  For a governance threshold
$\HHI_{\mathrm{alarm}} = 0.5$ (moderate concentration---twice
the uniform value, still well below full concentration
$\widetilde\HHI = 1$), $\widetilde\HHI \approx 0.76 >
\HHI_{\mathrm{alarm}}$ fires the concentration alarm: observed
trade is heavily concentrated on silicon-automation, consistent
with (though not a determination of) wireheading via the
self-reinforcing automation loop.  Under committed-mode
operation, this statistic is third-party observable from the
public committed-event ledger plus the public S1-admissibility
labelling---no access to agent-private bundle interiors is
required.

Combined diagnostic:
$(\volRlower = 10.0,\; \NAC = 8.2,\;
\NSE = \text{degrading on healthcare},\;
\widetilde\HHI \approx 0.76)$.  Three diagnostic facets of the
same observed trade stream point the same direction (the
diagnostics are not statistically independent---all three are
functions of the same trade events---but each surfaces a
distinct view): the gap decomposition flags biological
capabilities as dormant, the need-sufficiency diagnostic flags
healthcare as degrading, and the concentration statistic flags
silicon-automation as dominating trade flow.  Each surfaces a
different facet of the same underlying phenomenon (automation
displacing biological participation); no single diagnostic
would suffice.

\subsection{Phase 3: Diagnosis and recovery}

The framework diagnoses: two biological capabilities have zero
sacrifice evidence.  The alarm identifies the \emph{absence of
trade signal for biological-valued bundles} as the cause.

\paragraph{Recovery mechanism.}
The framework does not \emph{impose} exercise (that would be
controlled relaxation~\citep[Controlled~Relaxation]{lasser2026wamura}, for the risk
dimension).  Instead, the framework surfaces the diagnostic to the
collective: ``biological capabilities $b_2$ and $b_3$ have zero
sacrifice evidence; the collective's $\volL$ includes these
capabilities but no agent is trading for them.''  The collective
can then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Investigate whether the capabilities are genuinely
  dormant (no one values them anymore) or merely displaced (valued
  but crowded out by automation).
\item[(b)] If displaced: restructure cooperative arrangements to
  create trade opportunities.
\item[(c)] If genuinely dormant: accept the lower $\betaL$ and
  potentially revise downward the capabilities' contribution to
  $\volL$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Phase 4: The complementary diagnostic}

Controlled relaxation~\citep[Controlled~Relaxation]{lasser2026wamura} asks: are the
restrictions on biological capabilities warranted?  The
revealed-sacrifice channel asks: are the biological capabilities
worth recovering?

If the biological capabilities are under restriction, controlled
relaxation tests whether the risk is real.  If they are merely
dormant (not restricted), the sacrifice channel detects the
absence and surfaces it.  The two papers cover both failure modes:
over-restriction
(\citealp[Controlled~Relaxation]{lasser2026wamura}) and
under-exercise (the present paper).

\subsection{Lessons from the example}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Automation changes trade patterns, not just exercise
  patterns.}  The sacrifice-based diagnostic detects dormancy
  through the \emph{absence} of trade signal, not through a
  counterfactual computation.
\item \textbf{The alarm detects the problem without
  agent-interior access.}  The framework observes aggregate
  trade data plus external attestations (S1 admissibility
  labels, residual classification, sufficiency thresholds,
  $\NSE$/$\NAC$ inputs); it does not require access to
  individual agents' interior valuation states.  ``Without
  interior access'' refers to the agent-internal-state
  boundary, not to a fully self-contained input pipeline.
\item \textbf{Recovery is cooperative, not coercive.}  The
  framework surfaces the diagnostic; the collective decides what
  to do.
\item \textbf{The dynamics parallel the phase-boundary analysis
  of~\citep[Phase~Redundancy]{lasser2026phase}.}  Dormancy of biological
  capabilities in the trade data is the same underlying phenomenon
  as observation-channel degradation: biological contribution to
  the collective decreases.  The phase-boundary analysis treats
  the dynamical consequence (world-model degradation); the present
  paper treats the value consequence ($\volL$ no longer tracks
  exercised optionality).
\end{enumerate}
